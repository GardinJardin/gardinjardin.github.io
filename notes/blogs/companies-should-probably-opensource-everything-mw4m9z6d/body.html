Since the future of software is inevitably going to be AI reverse engineering everything and anything, obviously value comes from business metrics like data, permission, physical metrics, liability, talent, etc.

I'd go so far as to suggest that it's now good working practice for software companies to opensource their core engine, and make it as easy to locally use as possible, to make sure that they have the things that actually give their company value in 2026.

Obviously don't open source anything that you're reasonably confident AI can't reverse engineer.

These are just my thoughts, I'm not giving any sort of advice, and definitely don't make a serious business decision based off the musings of an 18 year old lol. But I definitely think this is where things are going, and good practice is going to look like that because core software engines have zero value now.
