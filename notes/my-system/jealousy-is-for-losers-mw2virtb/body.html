I admit, I’ve missed out on the chance to become a ‘prodigy’ of any kind.

But honestly?

I don’t care.

Insecurity isn’t something I tolerate.

Because there is always going to be someone smarter, richer or more talented than you.

I don’t want to become a man who spends his life crying because of perceived ‘inferiority’.

I’d rather be a man who figures out how to solve any problem that he wants to solve, regardless of difficulty, anxiety, insecurity, or anything else.

Because when we’re all at the end of our lives, bent over a crane, none of us are going to care about how many people we ‘surpassed’. The only thing I’d care about is how many adventures I went on, how many risks I took, and how hard I worked to do anything I wanted to.

And life is so much funner, so much free-er, when you're motivated by genuine interest and passion, than competition. These days, I just use other people's accomplishments as objective data on how I can improve, unemotionally, which has unexpectedly turned other people's success from something I dreaded to something I'm excited about, because it raises the bar on what I can do.
