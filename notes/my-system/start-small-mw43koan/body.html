When starting a new habit make it as simple as possible.

I tried to do muscle training for years, but I tried to first find the optimal set of exercises, which got difficult because of the amount of research involved, information overload, sequencing, etc.

Now I just do pushups every time im free. today i decided to do situps instead, but only situps.

No reason you can't slowly add more exercises.

Who cares if it's not optimal? I think a good motto to have is "start before planning".

there's also no rule saying you have to exercise every part of your body equally. every body is different and is allowed to look different.
