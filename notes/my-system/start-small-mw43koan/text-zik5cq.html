I've dreamed of writing fantasy or fiction books since I was, maybe 7?
But I never got very far, for one blasted reason: I had fun imagining the cool, or sad, or aura hype scenes, but writing the beginning of the book has been a blocker for years.
The solution: I literally just started writing the scenes I want, whenever I want, regardless of book number, chronology, or world building. Hell, I write scenes before I even know the names of the characters!
And I STILL don't have the beginning of the book written! It's something that I just can't figure out. If I write enoughs scenes to actually consider writing a full book, I'll probably force myself to grind out the intro but until then I'm having fun doing the easy stuff first
