Energy is my north star.

It’s the fundamental constraint on civilization, life and 21st century geopolitics.

Wars have been fought over the stuff.


But why?

Why can’t we look forward to a world where energy isn’t a constraint? Where it’s as abundant and useful as air?

Such a world would be a utopia.

This is what I think:
- Humanity can and should unlock more energy than it can possibly use

- We shouldn’t have to destroy the environment to unlock more and more of it. The world isn’t an ELA book. We can eat our cake and have it too.

- If humanity does unlock it, the importance of every single problem we face collapses to triviality.
